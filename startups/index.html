The engagement process

A deliberate path from idea to delivery.

  1. 01

    Discover

    Understand the business idea, target audience, objectives, constraints, and existing resources.

  2. 02

    Define

    Clarify product requirements, prioritize the MVP, and establish measurable milestones.

  3. 03

    Architect

    Select appropriate technologies, design the system, and identify technical dependencies and risks.

  4. 04

    Build & Iterate

    Implement prioritized functionality, validate integrations, test workflows, and adjust based on findings.

  5. 05

    Prepare to Launch

    Evaluate technical readiness, resolve critical blockers, document the system, and establish a plan for continued development.
